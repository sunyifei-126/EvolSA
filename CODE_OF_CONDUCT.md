# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith,
criticize ideas rather than people, disclose conflicts, and treat safety concerns seriously.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate release
of private vulnerability details, or misrepresenting experimental evidence. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. They will review reports promptly,
minimize disclosure, avoid retaliation, and take proportionate action.
